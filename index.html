Research

trustworthy ML
Algorithmic Recourse
When a model makes an unfavourable decision about a person, recourse asks what that person could realistically change to get a better outcome — and how to make such recommendations actionable, reliable and fair.
language models
Post-Training Techniques
Adapting pretrained language models after pretraining — through supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on curated demonstrations and reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) — to make them more useful and better aligned.
